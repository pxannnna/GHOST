About this animation & sources

This is a presentation, independent of the event hub. The camera journey illustrates a scenario; it is not a calculated location. Aircraft recordings and visible spectral features have been reported by the team; location matching is still to be validated. The city and campus close-ups are schematic. Room ranking, echoes, people and Wi-Fi traces are illustrative. Motion is not a specific gesture, a fall or a confirmed request for help. No emergency notification is sent.

Land outlines: Natural Earth, public domain. Campus context: University of Edinburgh. Building geometry: the project's existing floor-plan model, including measured and estimated dimensions. Arrow keys change scenes; Space plays or pauses. Animation pauses when the tab is hidden.
